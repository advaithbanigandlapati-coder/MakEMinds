Mecanum chassis, v2

Rebuilt after states: lower centre of gravity, a wider wheelbase, and bracing that survives defense. Field-oriented control on the driver hub means the rookie drivers stopped losing heading under pressure.

Two-stage cascade

Three presets on a tuned PID loop. It went from overshooting into the hardstops to settling in 220 ms with no oscillation — three tuning sessions, documented in the notebook.

Compliant-wheel intake

Printed compliant wheels on a sprung arm, forgiving on approach angle. This is the subsystem carrying straight into BIOBUZZ, where Pollen rewards fast repeatable pickup.

AprilTag pose fusion

Limelight 3A tag estimates fused with wheel odometry, which is what let us ship four autonomous routines in nine days instead of guessing at dead reckoning.
