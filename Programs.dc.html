Our flagship competition. Design, build, code a robot. Compete from regionals to worlds.

Team 23786 has competed in FTC since 2023 — three full seasons across CENTERSTAGE, INTO THE DEEP, and DECODE. We design with CAD, fabricate with hand tools and 3D printers, and code our control systems in OnBot Java and Android Studio.
