A design review where every voice gets equal weight on every decision
takes three hours and decides nothing. Here's the structure we settled
on after two seasons of bad meetings.

## Rule 1 — A single decision-owner per review

One person owns the decision. They're not the loudest voice, they're
the person on the hook to make the call by end of meeting. They
explicitly say "I'll decide" at the start. Everyone else gives input,
not vetoes.

## Rule 2 — Three rounds, then commit

- Round one: silent two-minute write-down of concerns + alternatives.
- Round two: round-robin reading aloud, no responses yet.
- Round three: open discussion, capped at 15 minutes.

After that, the decision-owner commits. We track decisions in a public
notebook so the team can revisit them next season.

## Rule 3 — Disagree, commit, document

If you disagreed, you say so, you say why, you put it in the notebook,
and then you commit to the chosen direction. Nobody is allowed to relitigate
a decision in slack at 11pm.

## What changed

DRs used to run 90+ minutes. Now they're 35. Decisions stick. We've
overruled past calls only twice, both with new evidence, both
documented.
