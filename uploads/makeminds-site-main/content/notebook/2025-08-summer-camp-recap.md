Eight weeks. Eighteen middle schoolers. Two mentors, four team members,
and a borrowed FLL kit per pair. No budget beyond snacks. Here's the
post-mortem.

## What worked

- **Pair programming on the SPIKE Prime app.** Two kids per laptop
  meant they corrected each other faster than we could.
- **A weekly demo at the library.** Showing off Friday's progress to
  parents kept attendance up — nobody wants to miss demo day.
- **The "break it on purpose" Wednesday.** Twenty minutes where every
  team had to introduce a bug into a working robot, then trade with
  another team to debug. Single biggest skill jump of the camp.

## What we'd cut

- The two-hour mission-planning lecture in week one. It was theoretical
  and they hadn't built anything yet. Should have been a 20-minute
  intro after week three when they had context.
- Trying to teach Python alongside SPIKE-Prime app blocks. It was too
  much. We'll start Python in week five next time.

## Carry-over to comp season

Three of the campers asked about joining FLL teams. Two are signed up.
That alone justified the program.
