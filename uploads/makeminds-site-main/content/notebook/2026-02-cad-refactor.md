After the states defensive match where we tipped at 17 seconds, the team
agreed in five minutes: v1 was too tall and too narrow.

## The numbers

- v1: 18″ wide, 19″ tall, COG at 9.4″
- v2: 17″ wide, 14.5″ tall, COG at 6.8″

## What we kept

- The mecanum geometry (it was never the issue).
- The cascade lift core — just reduced stage 3 by 5″.
- All the wiring topology; only the chassis plates changed.
